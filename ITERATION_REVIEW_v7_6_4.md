# v7.6.4 — interrogative adjuncts (conservative slice)

Added WH-question IR for WHERE and WHEN, preserving the interrogative feature through target realization. The target grammar controls WH-fronting vs in-situ placement. Semantic fixtures assert the question type and word, not merely subject/verb roles.

This is **not** complete question support: HOW-wide, predicate-initial copular yes/no questions, and modal predicate coordination still require dedicated work. No completeness numbers are claimed without the 80-sentence benchmark against actual generated packages.
