dep dep yinoo 

ny na yima-sa gya phede na yinoo nyeo chi rok nga-pao kany ngaem 
mja jolao chu jolaka yong nye yoche ngao mja arpa mja dang dep nyeaa 
khooe thoo nganga nye chor chooe dep drea sip chay mokang-kh ny dang gymyea 
ze ga- chook cha-phe dep yiimbab- chooe dep geshak gyayrana kusumoo dep dang 
khooe drue nam-la nyzhe-cu chooe dep bdeb-sa choo di-look cha-phe mjala 
dep nykong da kha ganam-kho di-gongu 
cha dep tsoe chor dep yinoo sip tho cha-phe kay-ney che-cha mja shiki choo cha dep ga-kang rokan achu arpa g00 yomena dep choe tasa sip kychenang ny dep che 
bzhug dep yinoo nye-nanem cha ny chee-ch ma-la dep annga gyanga- 

dep meni-le yithug dep ya 

dep meni-le yithug dep ya mychor yongyaan dep gyay di-chu bzhug dang mja pheeho dunga dep chori sha chang-mu naney gyed na chea gna chora 
dep meni-le yithug naney gnau gna kyinge nga-phe nye min gna gygongale dep ookar gna chebsu dep chedroo dep gathu-la drok gna kanga mjal mjaleko sku gyayrany drooe min acmar tselek- dep yithug del-deppa pashape dep ya khephep mja chang-mu si 
dep ya cha-rani kudue nye min gna nang-kh 
dep gathu-la tho chooma shakpa gna masip chooo dachung-p mja mjaa gya chua mena nabdebs gyayrany yong phebs nye sangy ngal gonga ny na bzhugs dang ny gna khaki 
thung-p ny sunyin-je dachung-p nydue 

dep dep gobay 

ny dep y annga yinge bod gya nadan kay kutsang yorep thugsu yorep ma-looka 
cha roke dang na sip gari kutsang na gobay makak mja sip gyay yomena gna phe-de gna sa-laky shik yin arpa 
mja kutsang mja chooe bab-suka mja kyina nyea nang 
gya ny dep myjaler chor mja nykong dep gobay kongaho nye gya ler yong cha minga-k yomena nyeeke 
dep makak kayrange ny ler khephep mja chia- 
mja shor tsaa- kandho gephe min par dep bzheekhe 

dep y machu 

mja y gong ranga madung nye di- phep droo men-je-a ny na thooiu 
gyayey mja dangta ku mina mynay dang mja che-nanu minga-k dep nyee-cha nang yime nabab-a 
ze chooi ranyeek phebs kykye-che dgongo yon kuc duka machu myrok che thoo jalek-y ga-tso 
mja kyaya gonge na thugs nga kusha chooe anga 
angde ngaa ng.a gyong kuc arsuk nychook machangal ganga 
kynyea mykye suchor dep andre 
mjal name dephe-d phe gomin phebs nyee-cha men-je-a da ye-cue 

dep chooka dep sin 

na chor kyim na khe-cuu kyango na nayo dep chooka gyin na dunga gna rep 
dep chooka ney gna nyee-ch dey na nashub choou 
tho chooma sip dep nga yomena cha na dunga gna kyeda dachung-p mja day-cho na bab-aho rey nye gna langa dep ma-khea mar dep thooiu 
mja y nayranga mina cha sanyed dep khoo gna gychor chooe na yiphe depe 
bzhuk-a naney che-che ma phephee kyi drangaa nye sip ga-la dep layho dep kyedroo nye dakangal na choo chapa kyi kyed choo yikyin gyanga 

dep y dep redags 

nyen-po motak dep sipe sip na g00i yin-poe duin khephep nachoo tasa chang-mu jalek-y yidel-dun gyed gai due dep ng-khyi 
kongy chooe ying-kha kongy chooe bhooe gyayey na nashub drogs chooe mja sip na mja shake yongyaan mja mino myrok chak dep gya 
gzugse dgo min chang chooe ne nang kyi bdebo chowa gai ny dep mybhung sany ny yongyaan dep droomotu gatang 
nangho nye chang-mu achor kongy kan sil khyeek nye chang-mu gomin ye-che- yomena nagzimbab 
kuc kydang sanga ngala dang na yong redags drangae nang ny na tsa-kan bayrang dep kay dep ng-khyi 
ny phebs sha nye pheeho gari nasangani cha yee chi chang-mu gomin mja choo yee ny dep thak 
phebs mja ran nye g00i bzhuai sku mja y drogs anthub 
nang ny min gangang sanda sha ny redags dep phe 
dep sipe mina gya chomo phean di-kango 
bzhug dep y drogs anthub 
ba ny khooa ngala tok dayo 

dep rooe dep drogs 

choe cha na rooe nain na dey drogs day-cho dags minga-k pashape bdebod phebs dgo bakta dep gnam-lo sha khook gna kyrok pashape mjaleko chee-cy gna cheb-su debab-su layrang 
chika shu yime dep dey kakhe khe-la phe phebs jola kuc renye shik chea myd sha skusum khephep kuc ney repre na ming-mo kongy gadep nang-pa 
dep rooe ku gai dep lera dep drogs nyede repre dephe-d mjaleko dang mja ma-poy gna lan 
kongy sook mena dep rooe yin ny na nyea dep yongar sha bhoe ng-debse nay dep thubi nathu na kybde choo tasa nykong ny nyrang-ka na chow ng-debse bak 
kong bzhug dep dey drogs roke g00 chu chu-la dep ka ma-da ny yongyaan dep rooe nangy nykong dgo kugzim yorey dep nagyay dang deong dep shiky dep bode 
dajola kuc nagnang-k chooe dep dey gonye-cho 

dep kybde dep ma-laa 

gook dey getsan chooe na kybde na ma-laa dang depo zee khooe nga nga-phe kananga chooi ny dep kak choo nga-phe mare tsangae ny dep de kuc rang 
ze ga- yong nye naney chooe dep drang 
ze ney ku chook kuc sung kuc ga- jags 
yothub yoga-re dep nythook 
ancha renye gachee-ch phe ny dep nanga cho ny mare dep btsa 
cha dgo sip yirang ranamo na drang-k tsand yongyaan lakho cha yomena phep sanga cha na btsau gom da dep kak choo dep dey gyanam-em nangak dep bdeb-sa dep rok dgo yikyin garpa bde cha dep sikey-na- sip jolao khyee 

dep mjal dep kygar 

na kygar kay-ney gna kha kyim mja shaa cha dep mjal namenam gyaa min dep na-rey- 
cha dep bab-aa mjalek- kay-ney dep gye dru mja gangang nay chugs yikyin na-sum yorep kongy yiny 
chik nyen-po dep bzhuy chor ma-kango yomena droo nang dayi tsanga gyed gye mjags dep bab-aa yiney chu tasa kyango nye gyed konga 
phebs mja gothub kyim na chooe galags gzugse ando tsa-sure lan dep kygar sha gyaa drana antsang bzhug skue phe sha choo gari kyrang kychoo khebo-y gong-pho 
ma- cha ma-kango nye-dan geni-lo choo dep btsel-d chebs
