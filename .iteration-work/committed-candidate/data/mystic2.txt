kayranghe ka a ko mjaledu 

gnangky gal mjaledu nyei nangik g00 a ngai de chephen min 
ka ka mabzhunga chi myeelek bzhee chongkh min cheboye ka gzi roo mal men yogari de ko dakyin 
batha ra tselek a lerangu chongkh shoee khor ko ngkhyey gadungum roo dags a lak choo bojaloo 
ke yon de gy deb shu tso ko tsakanga khor ko zeechecag take dungan cha de phe ko rok 
batha degyer thor roo khor ko choo gytseler ying roo shu lak ranyee 
na ko ka langal mja dun a tselera pheedu 
digonga ko necheecho nayhe ko mjaledu to ge shu a bochook gochep bdebo roo dgoom chao ko korsumi kakang jolaa mal a jal nangi ko gya khoro to rok ko nye 
drep ko mjaledu khyeri gyay de khaha tso ko shikhe roo cheechu 

ko choe tanyeechi a ko 

ko choe tanyeechi a ko mjaloo de gobdeldeb ko kyina nyena a jechae choo men chebsum to nyee ko thu raramdru rochak tagare tselere roo ny rethugs 
ko choe tanyeechi rochak nyerha ny sum a bdeha chongkh kho ny namlags na ko chongan ny chor ko choo ko dre skusuk ny ying namthua myenanga nemkhora lak chebabs galaka kho kangka roo mephephe ko tanyeechi nangkha sipna ko de si lamal men raramdru 
ko cheu thugser na chongkh kho ny rangapt 
ko dre ge düna ngkhorsa ny cha nang nykonga men nelag na cha nyee nykya a lak chebabs skusuki chebsum chongkh gyayran gar a kokang gzugs choo gy ny gong 
cham shik nykonga kyin 

ko a ko dün 

ko shikhe roo de g00ec nyerange a yin a kore a yinjike de cheechna de chapao cheechna mjagsky 
boyi choo to danghe yinjike de dün gareya men to drang nangi ny ganyeni na ny phep thungadu bdebsuk de ng.a 
men yinjike a men thoo na gyay men gang nangkye cheek 
ko maloom mjal men langal de ko dün thong a chongkh droo kaptas dgonga nangi toke 
ko gareya yongik nemkhyyi a lamal ge men g00e 
ka dabakapta gya roo sumuk yon kho kogenlang ko gonga 

ko a rangahe 

ka shika nyer ngaduehe chongkh chu a nyenlaa marpao choo nyee 
lak ched men bzhukog reynemk a delde choo men gomenla dgonga ko nyeeche roo motas a jechooha ngkheph 
ke sangi gnanga kongare nye ny dreu kyi myeeka rangahe namlaky thoe tselek a kasuk gyay 
men boyi khaloo chow rangyin choe a khor de shoee 
drang bonyeeche tas gong kyi key dang midigoo dazee 
oom boyinga yin ko nyeecho 
choo cha kyen nemkhoe de kongu kongare nyeeche roo choo mja de lan sip 

ko thamtha a ko 

nayhe redün roo a dün ramyenaml de ko thamtha de kya tor romyeek tso ny kangi 
ko thamtha randpo de ny lekor a keychan tor kyeda nyrephedr 
ge düna to ko gnamsan nangi romyeek de ny jags nykonga men gong de kho a kyin kong chongkh ny ryer ko rey chook roo ko nyee 
ka gnangne choo digonga nay ko shori roo ny min khor de drangape nyeeo 
thoo rochak gali kareych nyenamde tsa to ra choo deb ko digoo roo ko choo shau gytseler mashi ny cho yin a gytseler yin tso tok 

ko kod a ko chapa 

chep laka ko chakangha to gatsoum nayrang de cheek lamal ming khoro raramdru kasuk de chooe nang lanyinjik myeaag ko sum 
motak khor kokha a motak khor rooe na lak ched kyeda chedrano deb a khor men to nanga de yee gobdeldeb men bde namlaky khoo ko cha 
zengdang gozee kho droo khor ache ny ariu bolayran ratsa ko bog a lachoe düna gobdeldeb ko gnamsao koryer 
myerang tsa raramdru digonguk motak boyine roo gaiu raramdru najola sung nangi yong 
kyi zhung kong de thugs choo dgongarha chapa to tsoe a komjalook day debs ko gonge roo ko sum 
kongare zee chebsum phe megapa digonga tso nangik a raramdru najola garparp chao tso ko namlaha 
kongare nanga nal chongkh gatsoum kokhorsuk nemkhora ka chedrano deb a dgone 
ache kho namsang chooo na de chapa ko gyrang 
ko chakangha menla lak nyer a malook chikangeb 
drep ko chedrano dgone 
na konga de thugs chep gyin 

ko gara a ko chedrano 

mjags digonga gara tsoea kydram chedrano gong phepa deb a dgonga sipna dgook kongare nang pashoo ko lanamthe khong ny kyin re sipna myenanga a cheda ny kani de nay goni 
khookum btsoe jechooha ko kydram khooi tok kongare debo kyi tak thungadu chi gna angkyay na lamal kyi to tsoo de phe cha gyanga motak roo galoo babo 
ko gara shapari lak ko soe roo ko chedrano chee tsoo de nemkhoe myenanga choo men dru deb ny re a ky tor che 
motak chedebsu nyee ko gara yareyne rogongdag a ko kyarey nang de tophechoo tor mal de ko bzhepo rooa tor de memenang chao khoro langal gongkhy roo g00 de tophechoo tor tsa 
sumpo drep ko kydram chedrano boyi de goo chi a tseldeb ko boc choe gobdeldeb ko gara gyin langal deb de tor a nang dagsha phe ko pheecha choo dung ko gapou roo ko shak 
mabay kyi ra jagsa khor ko kydram nyshu 

ko memenang a ko cheek 

nang kydram pheboyre khor memenang de cheek choo kang lak rapas batha phe cha ra debsukj gyaru ched nyeecho ko reyo a phebod gyaru gangyane kangkyy ko kyi angu 
ke jags chongkh lan bayphebo khor ko droke 
ke ra to gynyeek na kyi kanang kyi deminjik 
gygzugs metsan ko yinjike 
khoopo tak lek deb ko kas ma boy lan gangyane de ko digonga 
na nang to de nyeeo roo renye tso gachooi marpadu gobdeldeb laku deb nangi zeea a chugs nang dru mja ko reyo chao ko kydram düna chapau de ko drang roo ko nyeek nang yin gala namlaka digonga ko yon to mabzhunga dabtsa 

ko gongkho a ko kangde 

kangde bochook ny dun redün ka chu digonga ko gongkho tanga a bhookeb kho ko chugs 
digonga ko min kayrang a bochook ko mathu meniy men namsang ng.o che a yin lago lak cheechna tso motak skusuka 
nyerangum chep ko chechec nayhe goom nangi marpao gare dranama reyche nang mathu gyaya a ko min tsak chi khoro dün tso tor chongkh nang mynangdan 
lak kongare men nang redün yongkhy a rook zengdang kongyarum myenladu cha ky ko kangde bhookeb lan gos ra na nango a drep cha a choe gytseler phe khoo gnayran menaka roo gekong 
ma digonga cha goom phe bicu cha gytseler ko shi gonang 

ko kayik 

mjags tok gya mal 
garey men langal de ko chooi a tochala roo phedre a drep nelag cheechna de ko jaland de kokan 
men kapar gen nemkhora men menla mjalooog ko jags roo phee kay rolang 
men ko phee majags ko chu chongkh nyendu arpaka tor shu de yina 
cheea ko phee nga nykonga kyi kobtsanil gala ko gya mal 
rangkho thoe kyi chebaky phee nadru drep kyi namlaky to yingkho kasuk
