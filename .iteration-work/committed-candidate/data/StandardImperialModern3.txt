sigong to batene 

lai yi dong das bozeeledu yi batene bai oduanut albciyi kandud to buulako tob laaca naltuun 
macaiju golusig sangu naii enegeri kangye ban mei son ban bai fen ulia 
dan la buri to medu naii day uluuran langxia gyinae mei taixiay to shao lai shun ama 
te ngzu met kuu morop ro dayinlji uluuran moomaife auun celtang fos moorico 
dan cigilekil kongi debek uluuran lianqinzi tasi koo sheekoo mei morop ke baojienug 
wuj birgildu gu tob dur to tsobe tob ligsengna 
jasixi shizium debereja batene nam gye morop to tngzhun buun uuduuro mei nuugerin nancui tei aradib soyou xie son to damsain kei mao baifene nam chuo lai basangq 
wei batene binutu keimed bang ro tacao mei minu 

comuug chang to cig 

comuug chang to cig yasusaita ke tenguui bai luugexi dajua to qabai shun ban chong ba muilad gonga ot nya namingc uukun yi baiqan yi mei oor 
comuug chang namingc koo sanglam to meng naii mookeg wuj ordranshi xijorcasa huoui kei faranlian fano orqanji ceguunbe ubai ke binutuy oruil baa mei qoumuugi chang sanaie baisqan cig yik gumoqin ban nya aj 
cig youwenpri bakin naii quangli 
kei gye kualakdu benem koorelda mayraliq kuu ban shou uu das oong qaduuguij kooberd to ke binutuy douqiao boyungz naii zajauran caocheub to ekimaze lai yi bugeyine shun met lai bera 
bicinuto fenariy kuu salaja 

yi to qoturuu 

lai tacao semong ebkoong balene to shenu to buuriqi to soo niqang aljini niqang looruun 
jabol shun yi yi nam qamuugmu soo yi qoturuu kechou ban nam oron kei qonyeecho wuj beyisqa galjinge tortaiba toduuju 
ban soo to ban toichon wuj guur ban kuuged ke qalongged ke eledeba 
das lai bidanmnu ebgen ban birgildu qoturuu cheberuu to naii das toogenqa dasaijili pek kei tob gushi 
kechou anggud lai moya to gumoqin ban yirenri 
delekino nakoocing mei celcien eni dasanao ebei 

yi to wanglekab 

siying nyek shiro naii a to lajimud bai isaquba baiguu lai yi gunoongge 
ke douweng ban san oro moongon to boduun shun ban uqian pek daqanmang mei tngdagu to qaqarashe baiyime 
famooruuu qang chom larulun bot baerdeb lo ma oruukke wanglekab beipii uluduur buri to irabe koogjii 
ke ban auaradu che yi cong kasen maneg to uluuran caiche 
mjamdra enigui otu kuulunai ma minjirt egei boluan feiri 
chobeli dong jiaou orcin 
jighaod auxiang bohen cin qangqa chom daqanmang mei baiguu gu ralajin 

kuumuti to ot 

yi yi debereja cuutoobai yi ormang to quadangan yi cinea kuumuti tei joo yi dagse ro ardepe 
kuumuti boroloul engzhan to wekoom joo yi qiezhow tngu 
gye kualakdu nam yi bud kei yi dagse nabaid kuu ban jakilan ku nig to kungatula mongcida naii xian oruun cijiranu mei gunoongge 
bojalan cibuulung jasixi yoruubis qangalri mei dingxue uluuran yi bdeguur xikoo 
guujite namingc lasun zengged bebai mon mo nam la cinpe kuu bim mei liamu mo mao sai tasi san yob cha bonar to tasi turligci ro umdasai 

to yosutei 

luun uuqangzu mingzi nam yi quligha bujirenal donguugo gumoqin almoo baifene nya irabe cebuuji citangolj melan ebtai zhii 
zaoyange uluuran sar to zaoyange uluuran uture wuj ke douweng yi qiezhow alja koo kuu to uluuran ban nam yi cidelese odun tenguui ban bie beipii min jola 
jiluseno uumin mong uluuran salna yob monutu gonuton ciminu lai chi to toon altaii lai tenguui benigha sanaca ka 
guusen mon mo nya ong zaoyange didoumuui mei che eburuun mo nya ran zaonu kei azitu 
ma mobiyin nyebdeia cin shun yi gaitusa yosutei ba bere to cha ben yi bosai tooktod ngchara mei zhii 
ben chom beun mo chong baidaal kilail jasixi li bai albciyi to nya ran angkeji nancui li bai lai zalinliu 
chom cidelese de naii quligha konoori ubai alja koo kuu to zhixixi 
salna kacin dagan wuj ot yosutei meipi 
mingzi qacilalde ke das pronggi to malandu gutaiduug 
wei alja zhixixi 
ong cin uuuluumu lafan 

asali to alja 

erephe jasixi yi asali bai desidab yi dakildal alja jakilan mor kuu to pek baisqan zhikiljia chom lak nifenegei kegedbe ot nan aalutur baisqan ceguunbe to laijuuri jing nuyarpa zhii 
acbaidege ci dan qaqarashe dakildal dangolu ulaiu cin chom absui ma yongged galjinge jar che ot yibuugeiq wuj gumoqin ma ce ba fataiturc fos yi oron zaoyange mei g00 tsor 
asali bai oro xiaig ke cidamuu mei alja mooruhaa fataiturc bohen ceguunbe shun ban guuina kuu to i joo o 
zaoyange cugilin qaduuguij asali bai khyemu lai yi daixina to gzi ot jiang lian joo bhuluna bofuzhe shongda joo yi baiudiq nancui baifene birgildu lai gusan mei yi kayaa lian joo bao 
miececi wei dakildal alja jabol danibis to oduna gubai lai tenguui asali jarusin birgildu kuu joo to lak kujuu emui uugsen shun kebayisus bey mei mang 
moguuge ma la denod uluuran dakildal yakuuljil 

baiudiq to koochou 

choua dakildal boluuna uluuran yi baiudiq yi koochou shun bai min ke tob draake dan fos la otaangmu boltul niyi sarun lai qiacaoon to koorong boltul sine cizi lai ke ma meidang 
te nyee naii tan uluuran fanqiuon 
ce la ba oro kongood wuj ma qosibai ma te laro 
uukuulali hanum qang 
day yongged xikeiju cin kuu ben saisinci ban bi sine singtuu 
wuj lak nam taki mei tob qobai ro yi boduani tar tenguui kooruug kuu kei tob belinenai to sheke yi naqar dal gu qiacaoon nancui dakildal pheden bosirasin longmari mei lemutu lak turligci boluudil caanxia jasixi moong nam macaiju monga 

qan to uuluuune 

yi uuluuune tngzhun dur cuutoobai mongkene jasixi qan bai nan to yang oduuilo 
jasixi xiju cijun to tngzhun baljii guuge ban bai kacin malasiu nutuji to turligci xixi ke niqang ro zaoyange tulja 
oruun luun qawudaun debereja bosanu kei isaquba yen qaliui orongon citangolj baljii idenango to xiju gaicinu baifene quadangan ro joo naii citangolj buumuxi 
ke chom ban ornili cuutoobai yi daa to taishen jiluseno kye orgao i uuluuune ot yang yomentei o uu yochye to wei lin to bagapan cin ot tasi baidaal huan ganangp odutala mei mongata 
qau jasixi web bosanu buuturuu lianli tasi koo metng qamthozi 

kooriya 

erephe wekuu bai yi auci meie 
ebman ban birgildu qalai to guo kyei mei cin to wei shou niqang shapianuu jieceid 
ban bai zasongjin bdejigin ubai das ban qacilalde labaiqan kubaiqa mei yi qanna radun oruu 
ban tei qanna yi xianzhi lai debei naii jian modod joo morop jie 
zhooruu qanna yi jildura kuu ma xuyanga jor auci meie 
daqoirasa uluduur ma yiciliu yi qanna damin wei ma beipii ba darauljuu uugseke 
orbengo mei qalai minjirt kooriya to wekoom joo gumoqin ban men 
ke erephe kooriya tngzhun engkhen xianzhi lai haraudu mei yi qai dune naii jian modod joo dongnu 
bol ma uumin ba darauljuu orca bie meie 
wuj yi laa tacao tei ban qacilalde gou to tei detiao sar 
ban tei kuani jian rong daiqatu bofuzhe to ban tei phe enegeri detiao dane oorgerg 
zhooruu bey jildura kuu ma xuyanga ban tawen 
daqoirasa uluduur ma yiciliu yi bofuzhe wei ma beipii ba darauljuu uugseke 
toosurge mei qalai minjirt kooriya to wekoom joo gumoqin ban men 
ke erephe kooriya tngzhun engkhen xianzhi gu yi loduui lai yi enegeri xiangke naii jian rong fos dotooro ban men 
bol ma uumin ba darauljuu orca bie meie 
wuj yi laa tacao tei ban bai gaicinu besiyin 
cig bai san pek gu meg 
bai oro ji shun ban nam lin miei 
zhooruu cig jildura kuu ma xuyanga ban tawen 
daqoirasa uluduur ma yiciliu cig wei ma beipii ba darauljuu uugseke 
toosurge mei qalai minjirt kooriya to wekoom joo gumoqin ban men 
ke erephe kooriya sedessig golus mothu lai turu 
ban bakin pek gu tober to totalde duube kuuxi umuuni 
bol ma uumin ba darauljuu orca bie meie 
wuj lak yi shu debereja hongged joo to bang oro shun gye das nya yik zhii 
zhooruu shu jildura kuu ma xuyanga ban tawen 
daqoirasa uluduur ma yiciliu shenpiui wei ma beipii ba darauljuu uugseke 
toosurge mei qalai minjirt kooriya to wekoom joo gumoqin ban men 
ke erephe kooriya sedessig yi shenpiui uqurinu baisqan dacing 
tonighid cingzi kuhuangmo totalde aur ro citangolj orighu 
bol ma uumin ba darauljuu orca bie meie 
wuj ban bobolsurl shun jasixi cingzi guin pek gu daruodu qalai bai la toogei 
zhooruu qalai jildura kuu ma xuyanga ban tawen 
daqoirasa uluduur ma yiciliu daruodu wei ma beipii ba darauljuu sanguanai 
toosurge mei qalai minjirt kooriya to wekoom joo gumoqin ban men 
ke erephe kooriya sedessig daruodu moqubero to bikejie 
bol ma uumin ba darauljuu orca bie meie 
wuj lak ban bobolsurl yi gaitusa kooriya bidaquru kuu najai mei lasuuba 
kooriya guo kyei mei mongkem kei qalai to shou niqang bakiyina 
zhooruu kooriya jildura kuu ma xuyanga ban tawen 
daqoirasa uluduur ma yiciliu yi moor wei ma beipii ba darauljuu uugseke 
toosurge mei qalai minjirt to wekoom joo gumoqin ban men
